About Me

Life Story, Values & Personal Reflection

Section A: Life Story & Personal Reflection

My name is Zarak Khan, and I am a Computer Science student at York University's Lassonde School of Engineering. Outside of classes, I am an avid weightlifter, play volleyball, and enjoy learning about new technology. I grew up in the quiet town of Port Elgin, which taught me to be naturally reserved with my communication to avoid small-town scrutiny. This independent mindset was tested when I missed the computer science POSt requirement at the University of Toronto. Facing disappointment, transferring to York gave me a fresh start. Instead of focusing only on strict grade cutoffs, I began developing practical skills outside the classroom, such as SQL and PowerBI, which eventually helped me secure my current co-op. My technical resilience comes directly from my mother. When I almost settled for an easier statistics stream out of convenience, she pushed me to continue pursuing programming. Her high expectations taught me that computer science requires the ability to overcome setbacks and stay committed to a long-term vision.

Section B: Values Perspective Reflection

My top priorities are financial security, workplace independence, active well-being, empathy, and optimism. While personality tests suggest reserved people prefer slower environments, I actually thrive in the fast-paced energy of a city setting. I also have a natural ability to read a room and understand people's emotions, which helps me collaborate with diverse coworkers. My values of optimism and persistence were heavily tested when I faced rejection from Western University while secretly retaking failed prerequisites. Despite an eight-hour weekly bus commute from Port Elgin to preserve a 10% lab grade, I carried my academic anxiety quietly. My optimism never broke. When my York acceptance arrived, my "Let's goooo!" moment showed me that refusing to give up can help overcome incredible pressure. Now, working at Bruce Power, my co-op has shown me that software development is more than writing code; it requires communication, teamwork, and continuous learning.

My Vision as an Engineer

My vision as an engineer is to become a computer science professional who uses technology to improve the experiences of the people and organizations it serves. I believe technology should do more than simply function correctly; it should be intuitive, accessible, and create meaningful value for those who rely on it. My goal is to combine technical expertise with strong communication, collaboration, and leadership skills to develop solutions that positively impact both individuals and the broader community.

I envision a future where technology simplifies complex problems, supports informed decision-making, and creates opportunities for people to learn, collaborate, and succeed. Just as engineers build bridges that connect communities, I view technology as a bridge that connects people with knowledge, opportunities, and innovative solutions. My vision is to help build those bridges by creating technology that empowers others and contributes positively to the organizations and communities I serve.
